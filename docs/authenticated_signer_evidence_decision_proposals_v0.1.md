# Authenticated Signer Evidence Decision Proposals v0.1

Status: `PROPOSALS_ONLY_NON_APPROVING`

Foundation: 126, workstream 1

Coverage: `LSOD-EVD-001` through `LSOD-EVD-010`

## 1. Review boundary

This document converts the ten authenticated-evidence decisions into concise operator-reviewable candidate packets. Every candidate is unselected and every decision remains `CANDIDATE_NOT_APPROVED`. Candidate comparison is not approval, implementation authority, proof verification, credential issuance, signer eligibility, or signer invocation.

The fixed repository constraints are not candidates: evidence is versioned, canonical, exact-bound, public-only, revocation-aware, fail closed, and subordinate to L28 Protocol v1.0.0. `coin.tx_validation.validate_transaction` remains canonical and mandatory. Authorization is not validation. Eligibility is not invocation.

## 3. Approval boundary and invariants

No packet is an approval record. Before approval, the named evidence, rationale, accountable authority, independent review, effective interval, supersession, and rollback/change-control fields from Foundation125 remain mandatory. Missing evidence fails closed.

Preserved exactly: hard cap `28000000`; emission ceiling `11130000`; historically mined `2824584`; treasury locked `500000`; circulating snapshot `2324584`; halving interval `210000`; reward schedule `[28,14,7,3,1,0]`; historical mined-through entry `100877`; next canonical height `100878`; coinbase-only issuance; consensus-derived canonical height; immutable historical evidence.

Bitcoin remains external evidence only. Production Bitcoin proof architecture, Bitcoin confirmation/reorg policy and count, observer quorum/independence, and signer implementation/runtime/deployment/activation remain `BLOCKED_REQUIRES_FUTURE_SECURITY_DECISION`.

Foundation126 authorizes no implementation, credential, verifier, signer, runtime, deployment, or activation.
