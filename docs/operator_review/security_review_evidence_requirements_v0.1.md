# Local Signer Security Review Evidence Requirements v0.1

Status: `SECURITY_REVIEW_PREPARATION_ONLY`

These requirements cover exactly the 29 Foundation126 `SECURITY_EXPERT_DECISION_REQUIRED` items. They define evidence expected by an independent reviewer; they approve no candidate and select no production value. Missing required evidence or an unresolved dependency yields `NOT_READY_FOR_OPERATOR_CONSIDERATION` and fails closed.

## 1. Common evidence package

Every item requires:

- exact LSOD ID and versioned source-artifact inventory;
- decision-specific threat model, trust boundaries, actors, assets, abuse cases, assumptions, failure modes, recovery conditions, privacy effects, and residual risks;
- candidate shapes and rejected alternatives without treating any candidate as approved;
- authenticated provenance for any supplied design, measurement, fixture, test, or review artifact;
- deterministic/reproducible conformance and adversarial evidence using fictional or disposable data only;
- traceability from threat to control, evidence, test, finding, remediation, and independent re-verification;
- reviewer identity, relevant qualifications, independence/conflict statement, methods, limitations, severity-rated findings, and disposition; and
- explicit confirmation that review evidence grants no implementation, runtime, deployment, or activation authority.

## 6. Evidence disposition rules

- Evidence must be version-bound and reviewed as a coherent bundle; fragments from incompatible versions cannot be combined.
- Offline fixtures, plans, and passing tests are supporting evidence only and cannot prove production implementation, deployment, or activation.
- A favorable reviewer recommendation does not approve a decision. The accountable operator must later create a complete decision record.
- Any unremediated critical/high finding, missing independent review, missing dependency, or ambiguous authority boundary produces `NOT_READY_FOR_OPERATOR_CONSIDERATION`.
- No reviewer may resolve `LSOD-GAT-001`, `LSOD-GAT-002`, `LSOD-GAT-003`, or `LSOD-GAT-004`; production Bitcoin proof architecture, Bitcoin confirmation/reorg policy/count, observer quorum/independence, and signer activation remain blocked.

## 7. Protocol, economics, and non-execution invariants

L28 Protocol v1.0.0 remains authoritative. `coin.tx_validation.validate_transaction` remains the mandatory canonical validator. Authorization is not validation. Eligibility is not signer invocation. Bitcoin is external evidence only. No review artifact may override issuance, supply, canonical height, validation, consensus, history, or settlement.

Protected facts remain exactly: `28000000`, `11130000`, `2824584`, `500000`, `2324584`, `210000`, `[28,14,7,3,1,0]`, `100877`, `100878`; coinbase-only issuance; consensus-derived canonical height; immutable historical evidence.
