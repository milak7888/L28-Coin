Status: `PROPOSALS_ONLY_NON_APPROVING`

Coverage: `LSOD-CUS-001` through `LSOD-CUS-012`

## 1. Review boundary

This document presents unapproved custody-control candidates for operator and independent-security review. It accesses or creates no key, wallet, signature, HSM/KMS, credential, secret location, runtime, RPC/network connection, broadcast, or settlement path. Every decision remains `CANDIDATE_NOT_APPROVED`.

No candidate may weaken L28 Protocol v1.0.0, canonical `coin.tx_validation.validate_transaction`, authorization/validation separation, eligibility/invocation separation, fail-closed custody state, or the prohibition on secret material in public artifacts.
