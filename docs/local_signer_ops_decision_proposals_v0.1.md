# Local Signer Operations Decision Proposals v0.1

Status: `PROPOSALS_ONLY_NON_APPROVING`

Coverage: `LSOD-OPS-001` through `LSOD-OPS-009`

## 1. Review boundary

This document presents unapproved candidate shapes for trusted time, audit durability, parser/resource limits, secure errors, monitoring, isolation, operations, and assurance. It starts or implements no time source, audit store, parser, monitor, server, runtime, network, database, signer, wallet, key, RPC, broadcast, settlement, deployment, or testnet. Every decision remains `CANDIDATE_NOT_APPROVED`.

Unknown operational numeric values are never guessed. They remain `PARAMETER_REQUIRES_MEASUREMENT`. All controls remain subordinate to L28 Protocol v1.0.0 and canonical `coin.tx_validation.validate_transaction`; authorization is not validation and eligibility is not invocation.
